# Solomon

## Register

Operate. Two adults use Solomon every day to see the house. The light screen is a warm household dashboard: cards, person colors, and a compact bottom nav.

## Purpose

A private household app: grocery and freshness now, lights, Blink, and systems later. Home Assistant stays paired to the hardware. Solomon is the screen.

## Constraints

- Three themes, chosen on Settings: light, navy, and dark. The choice is remembered.
- Food copy stays calm. No red alerts, no alarm badges, no "expired" wording.
- The grocery flows already built keep working. This record does not add features.
- Untrusted text (email, receipts) does not become visual chrome or a claim.

## Voice

Precise and quiet. Say what the household can do. Do not hype the model.

## Anti-references

Generic purple-gradient SaaS. Neon glow, glass panels, and a costume of monospace. An iframe of the Home Assistant dashboard. Red food alerts.
